    About the model
    A supervised regression model trained on historical food delivery orders. It predicts the target Time_taken(min).
    
    
    
      
    Data and cleaning
    • 45,593 raw orders, 45,162 after removing impossible distances (over 100 km).
    • Text such as “conditions Sunny” cleaned, missing values filled with median or mode, ratings capped at 5.
    
      
    Feature engineering
    Four coordinates become one distance_km via the Haversine formula, and the order time becomes order_hour. Traffic is encoded Low to Jam as 0 to 3; other categories are one-hot encoded and everything is standardised.
    
      
    Final model
    XGBoost Regressor tuned with randomised search (500 trees, depth 8, learning rate 0.01, subsample 0.9), trained on an 80/20 split.
